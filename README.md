## 🚀 Featured Work

### 🤖 Who Gets Misled by AI? *(in progress)*
**When people use AI advice, who benefits and who gets misled?** A study of how people's own skill, and the way AI is presented to them, shape whether they follow good advice or bad advice. Write-up coming soon.

### 🏎️ [Same Car, Different Driver](https://github.com/behalsrishti08/same-car-different-driver)
**Isolating human skill from machine performance.** Using **64,000 IMSA endurance-racing laps**, I compared teammates who shared the same car, so the car is removed from the comparison by design.
- Gold drivers look 0.17 s/lap slower than Platinum across different cars, but only **0.03 s/lap** (not significant) against a teammate in the same car: most of the apparent skill gap is the machine.
- The same "hold the machine fixed, measure the person" logic underpins how AI tools and self-driving cars should be judged.
- `mixed-effects models` `bootstrap` `causal inference` `Python`
